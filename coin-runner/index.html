How to play

  • Desktop: move with arrow keys / WASD, or move the mouse to steer. Press Space, Esc, or P to pause.
  • Mobile: drag the joystick at the bottom of the screen.
  • Collect gold coins for points. Avoid the red wanderers and orange chasers.
  • Power-ups: Shield blocks one hit, Magnet pulls coins in, Slow-Time slows every enemy.
  • The arena gets harder the longer you survive, and eases off if you're struggling.
